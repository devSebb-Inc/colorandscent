## Part 3: Actionable Design Decisions for Color & Scent

### Recommended Color Palette

```
Primary Colors:
  Deep Indigo:     #1A1A2E  (headings, primary text, nav)
  Warm White:      #FAF8F5  (background, cards)
  Blush Rose:      #E8B4B8  (accent, CTAs, hover states)

Secondary Colors:
  Sage Green:      #8FAE8B  (secondary accent, badges)
  Golden Hour:     #D4A574  (warm accent, sale prices)
  Soft Cream:      #F5EDE3  (section backgrounds, alternates)

Neutrals:
  Charcoal:        #2D2D2D  (body text)
  Medium Gray:     #6B6B6B  (secondary text, captions)
  Light Gray:      #E5E5E5  (borders, dividers)
  Pure White:      #FFFFFF  (cards, overlays)
```

**Rationale:** The palette bridges the warmth of Otherland/Homesick with the sophistication of Diptyque/Brooklyn Candle Studio. The blush rose (`#E8B4B8`) serves as the signature "color" in "Color & Scent" — memorable, feminine-adjacent without being exclusionary, and photographable.

### Typography Pairing

```
Headings:    "Playfair Display" (Google Fonts) — elegant serif, signals luxury
Body:        "Inter" (Google Fonts) — clean, modern, highly readable
Accent:      "Space Mono" (Google Fonts) — for scent notes, prices, technical details
```

**Why this pairing:**
- Playfair Display mirrors Diptyque/Brooklyn's serif elegance at zero cost
- Inter is the most readable web font, used by Byredo and others
- Space Mono for scent notes creates a "perfumer's notebook" feel
- All three are free via Google Fonts (critical for Printify/Shopify budget)

**Alternatives (if budget allows):**
- Headings: "Canela" or "Recoleta" (paid, more distinctive)
- Body: "Söhne" or "Untitled Sans" (paid, more premium)

### Product Photography Direction

**Style:** "Elevated Warmth" — clean but not clinical, warm but not rustic

**Shot List per Product:**
1. **Hero Shot** — Candle on clean warm-white (`#FAF8F5`) background, centered, soft shadows
2. **Lifestyle Shot** — Candle in styled interior (shelf, coffee table, bedside), warm natural light
3. **Detail Shot** — Close-up of wax texture, wick, label typography
4. **Scale Shot** — Hand holding candle or candle next to common object
5. **Scent Story Shot** — Abstract composition with ingredients (e.g., lavender sprigs, vanilla beans)

**Photography Rules:**
- Warm color temperature (3200K–4000K)
- Soft, diffused natural light (no harsh flash)
- Muted, earthy props (linen, wood, ceramics, dried flowers)
- No clutter — max 3-4 props per frame
- Consistent aspect ratio: 4:5 for social, 1:1 for product cards, 16:9 for hero

**Reference Brands:** Otherland (artistic), Brooklyn Candle Studio (clean), Apotheke (lifestyle)

## Summary: Top 5 Design Decisions

1. **Color palette:** Deep Indigo + Warm White + Blush Rose — bridges luxury and accessibility
2. **Typography:** Playfair Display (serif headings) + Inter (body) + Space Mono (scent notes) — free, premium feel
3. **Pricing:** $34 classic candle as hero, $14 wax melts as entry point, bundles for AOV
4. **Differentiator:** Color-coded scent collections with custom candle builder — no competitor does this
5. **Homepage:** Hero → Brand Statement → Featured Collection → How It Works → Social Proof → UGC → Email Capture
