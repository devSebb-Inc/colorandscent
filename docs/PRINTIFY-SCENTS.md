## Mockup Generator for Candles

### How It Works

Printify's mockup generator allows you to:
1. **Upload your label/design** - Create a custom label design and upload it to Printify
2. **Apply to candle products** - The design is automatically applied to the candle jar/container
3. **Generate mockups** - Printify generates multiple mockup images showing your design on the candle
4. **Download for listings** - Use these mockups for Etsy, Shopify, or other platform listings

### Custom Mockup Capabilities

- **Yes, you can create custom mockups** with your label designs on candle images
- Upload your label artwork (PNG, JPG recommended)
- The mockup generator applies your design to the candle jar
- Multiple angle/scene mockups are generated automatically
- You can download high-resolution images for your product listings

### Best Practices for Candle Mockups

1. **Label Design Dimensions:** Check each product's template for exact dimensions
2. **File Format:** PNG with transparent background recommended
3. **Resolution:** High resolution (300 DPI) for best quality
4. **Design Placement:** Consider the curved surface of candle jars
5. **Color Contrast:** Ensure your design stands out against the candle color
