## Design/PNG Mapping

> ⚠️ **User has PNG designs ready** — need to map them to products.

Each product needs a label design uploaded to Printify. The label wraps around the candle jar/tin.

**Design specs for Printed Mint candles:**
- Format: PNG, 300 DPI
- Dimensions: Product-specific (check Printify mockup generator)
- Must include: Brand name, scent name, burn time, weight, ingredients

**Label placement:**
- Glass/Amber jars: Front label (full wrap or partial)
- Tin candles: Lid label
- Frosted glass: Front label
- Gift set: Box design

---

## Mockup Generation

Printify has a built-in mockup generator:
1. Select product → upload label design → auto-generate mockups
2. Multiple angles/scenes available
3. Can download mockup images for website
4. Use mockups for product pages, social media, ads

**Recommendation:** Create label templates in Figma/Canva, then upload to Printify for mockup generation.
